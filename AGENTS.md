# KrishanuBuilds Anti-Vibe-Coding Design Manifesto & Guidelines

This codebase follows strict anti-vibe-coding, high-craft design and engineering principles established by KrishanuBuilds. Every AI agent, assistant, or contributor working on this repository must adhere to the following rules without exception.

---

## 1. Aesthetic & Color System
- **Zero Purple Gradients:** Never use purple, violet, fuchsia, or neon cyan gradients, halos, or glowing dropshadows.
- **Neutral Dark Craft:** Use deep, matte neutral dark surfaces (`#09090b`, `#111115`, `#18181b`), subtle hairline borders (`border-zinc-800` / `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and crisp high-contrast text (`text-zinc-100`, `text-white`).
- **Accent Restraint:** Accents should be intentional and functional (e.g., standard high-contrast neutral buttons, subtle emerald indicators for live status, or clean tech blue).

## 2. Component Geometry & Buttons
- **Zero Pill Buttons:** Never use pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) buttons or action triggers.
- **Geometric Radii:** All interactive buttons and input fields must use structured geometric corners: `rounded-md` (6px) or `rounded-lg` (8px).
- **Tactile Feedback:** Use crisp border transitions, clear hover states, and active pressed states rather than fuzzy diffuse glows.

## 3. Truthful Content & Copy
- **Zero Fake Reviews or Ratings:** Never display fake testimonials, fictional "L5 SRE" quotes, or fabricated 5-star ratings.
- **Zero Fake Metrics & Counters:** Never include fake telemetry (e.g. "99.8% satisfaction", "10,000+ happy engineers", "180ms neural latency").
- **Zero Vague Hero Copy:** Avoid empty AI buzzwords ("The future of X powered by AI", "Supercharge your workflow"). Write direct, concrete, benefit-driven technical copy describing real functionality.
- **Zero AI Slop Copy:** Write authentic, technical, human copy calibrated for software engineers.

## 4. Typography & Punctuation
- **Zero Em Dashes (`—`):** Do not use em dashes. Use colons, hyphens (`-`), commas, or separate sentences cleanly.
- **Zero Emoji Icons:** Do not use emoji icons (e.g. 🚀, ✨, 💡, 👋, ⚡, 🔥) in headlines, cards, buttons, or navigation. Use clean SVG icons (such as Lucide React) with precise sizing (`w-3.5 h-3.5` or `w-4 h-4`).

## 5. Visuals & Motion
- **Zero 3D Orb AI Slop:** Never use generic spinning 3D wireframe spheres, floating holographic discs, or cursor animations.
- **Authentic Product Previews:** Showcase real, interactive product interfaces (code editors, terminal runners, split-pane interview consoles, rubric breakdowns).
- **Restrained Motion:** Keep animations subtle, fast, and functional (150ms-250ms transitions). Avoid exaggerated floating or dizzying scroll parallax.

## 6. Pre-Launch Requirements
- **Custom Favicon:** Always maintain a custom, crisp SVG icon (`icon.svg`), never the default framework starter icon.
- **Legal & Compliance Pages:** Keep fully functional, accessible `/privacy` (Privacy Policy) and `/terms` (Terms of Service) pages linked in the footer.
- **Zero "Made with AI" Tags:** Remove all promotional badges or watermarks claiming the app was generated by AI.
